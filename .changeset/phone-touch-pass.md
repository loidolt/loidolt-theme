Fit controls to phones: a single-line select drops the system's clothes, which WebKit draws as a 28px pill whatever the box says, and draws its own chevron; NumberField hides the browser's spinner beside its own − and +; a disabled field reads as disabled; FileInput's button and "no file chosen" text match, and the button is 44px on touch. On touch screens swatches, list rows, the Dropzone's "choose files" link, page buttons, sort headers, stage labels, breadcrumb links and the folded map attribution all take a 44px press, inline radios sit further apart, and a chip inside MultiSelect keeps its size. A table that scrolls sideways shows a shadow on the side with more to see, and its headers stay on one line. On a phone Pagination collapses to Previous, the current page and Next instead of wrapping, the layer manager stops at half the map's height, and the media speed select keeps its box. Badges no longer wrap inside their pill. The select's chevron follows the writing direction and gives way to the system's arrow under forced colours, and the map attribution's (i) is drawn in the theme's ink, so it shows on a dark map.
